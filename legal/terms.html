Last updated: August 26, 2026

Program description

When you call (925) 396-7124 to place a pickup order, our automated ordering assistant takes your order over the phone. Before the order is submitted, the assistant reads the order back to you and tells you it will text the receipt to the number you are calling from. If you confirm, we send a single text message containing your order details, the subtotal, and your pickup number.

How you consent

Consent is given verbally during the call. The assistant states that it will text the receipt to the number you are calling from and asks you to confirm before the order is submitted. No text is sent unless you place an order.

Message frequency

One message per order. These are transactional messages only. We do not send recurring, marketing, or promotional messages.

Cost

Message and data rates may apply. We do not charge you for the message; your mobile carrier's standard rates apply.

Opting out

Reply STOP to any message to stop receiving texts. You will receive a single confirmation that you have been unsubscribed, and no further messages after that. You can still order by phone; you just will not get a written receipt.

Help

Reply HELP to any message, or email sahiel@openswarm.com.

Carriers

Carriers are not liable for delayed or undelivered messages. Delivery is not guaranteed; your pickup number is also read to you out loud on the call, so you do not need the text in order to collect your order.

Privacy

Mobile numbers are never shared with third parties for marketing. See our Privacy Policy.
