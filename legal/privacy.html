Privacy Policy

Phone ordering assistant for Anjappar Chettinad Cuisine, Dublin, CA
Last updated: August 26, 2026

This policy covers the automated telephone ordering assistant reachable at (925) 396-7124, and the order confirmation text messages it sends. The service is operated by Sahiel Bose.

What we collect

When you call to place a pickup order we collect:

  • The phone number you are calling from, provided automatically by the telephone network.
  • The first name you give for the order.
  • The items, quantities and options you order, and the order total.

Calls are processed by automated speech recognition in order to understand your order. We do not ask for and do not want payment card numbers, and payment is taken in person at the restaurant, not over the phone.

How we use your mobile number

Your mobile number is used for one purpose only: to send you a text message confirming the order you just placed, listing what you ordered, the subtotal, and your pickup number.

We do not share, sell, rent, or otherwise disclose mobile phone numbers or SMS consent to any third party for marketing or promotional purposes. No mobile information is shared with third parties or affiliates for their own marketing use. Phone numbers are disclosed only to our telecommunications provider (Twilio) to the extent required to deliver the message you asked for, and where we are required to do so by law.

Message frequency

One message per order placed. This is a transactional confirmation, sent only after you place an order and only to the number you called from. We do not send recurring messages, marketing messages, or promotional campaigns of any kind.

Message and data rates may apply.

Opting out

Reply STOP to any message to stop receiving texts from this number. Reply HELP for assistance. Opting out of texts does not prevent you from ordering by phone; you will simply not receive a written confirmation.

How long we keep it

Order records, including the name and phone number attached to an order, are retained only as long as needed to fulfil the order and to keep basic business records. We do not build marketing profiles from order history.

Contact

Questions about this policy, or requests to delete information we hold about you: sahiel@openswarm.com.
